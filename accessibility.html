Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

How this site is built

The site is plain HTML and CSS with a small amount of optional JavaScript. In practice that means:

  • Every page works with JavaScript turned off.
  • You can reach and operate everything with a keyboard alone, and the focus outline is always visible.
  • Headings follow a proper order, so screen-reader users can navigate by heading.
  • Text reflows without a horizontal scrollbar down to 320 pixels wide, and can be zoomed to 200% without anything being cut off.
  • Animation and smooth scrolling are switched off automatically if your system is set to reduce motion.
  • Body text starts at 19 pixels and gets larger on wide screens — never smaller. Line length is capped so lines stay easy to follow.
  • Black text on a white background throughout, a contrast ratio of 21:1 — far above the 4.5:1 minimum.
  • Every button, link and form box is at least 56 pixels tall, so they are easy to tap or click without precise aiming.
  • Links inside paragraphs are underlined, so you never have to rely on telling colours apart.
  • Nothing is loaded from a third party, so no external widget can break any of the above.
