Full dentures

A complete replacement for an upper arch, a lower arch, or both. A full denture does more than replace teeth: it supports your lips and cheeks, which is what stops the face looking sunken after natural teeth are lost.

We record the shape of your gums, how your jaws meet and how your lips move when you speak and smile, then set the teeth to suit your face rather than to a standard template. You see and approve the arrangement at the wax try-in, while everything can still be changed.

  • Premium or standard tooth moulds and shades
  • High-impact acrylic base as an option for heavy wear
  • Copy dentures available if you like the shape you already have

Partial dentures

For anything from a single missing tooth to most of an arch. A partial denture fills the gaps and stops the remaining teeth drifting into them, which is what makes small gaps turn into bigger problems over time.

The right material depends on your remaining teeth and how much support they can give:

  • Acrylic — the most economical, and easy to add to later
  • Cobalt chrome — a slim, strong metal framework, kinder to the gums
  • Flexible nylon — no metal clasps, useful where appearance matters most

Because you still have natural teeth, we make partial dentures to a written prescription from a dentist. If you do not have one, we will help you find a practice. See the FAQs for how that works.

Implant-retained dentures

If a lower denture will not stay put — a common problem once the ridge has resorbed — a denture that clips onto two or more implants is usually the answer. It stays in place when you eat and talk, and still comes out for cleaning.

We do not place the implants ourselves. We work with your implant surgeon: they place the fixtures, we design and make the denture that attaches to them, and we handle the ongoing maintenance such as replacing worn retention caps.

  • Assessment of whether existing implants can be reused
  • Locator, ball or bar attachment systems
  • Retention caps replaced as part of routine servicing

Implants count as teeth for regulatory purposes, so this work is always carried out to a written prescription from your dentist or implant surgeon rather than on a direct-access basis.

Immediate dentures

Made in advance from impressions taken while your natural teeth are still in place, and fitted on the same day they are removed. You never have to go out without teeth.

Gums shrink as they heal over the following months, so an immediate denture is a staging post rather than a final result. Expect a reline — or a permanent replacement denture — once healing has settled, usually around six months on. We price that in from the start so there are no surprises.

Repairs, relines and additions

Because the laboratory is on site, most repairs are done the same day — often while you wait. Bring in every piece, however small, and please do not try to glue it first: household adhesives contaminate the acrylic and can turn a simple repair into a remake.

  • Repairs — cracked or snapped bases, broken clasps, lost teeth
  • Relines — new fitting surface when the denture has gone loose
  • Additions — adding a tooth to a denture after an extraction
  • Cleaning — professional removal of built-up stain and tartar

Mouthguards and night guards

Custom guards are made from impressions of your own teeth, so they fit closely, stay put and let you speak — which is why they get worn, and boil-and-bite versions from a sports shop often do not.

  • Sports mouthguards — single or multi-layer, in your choice of colours
  • Night guards — for grinding and clenching, to protect teeth and ease jaw ache
  • Anti-snoring devices — following assessment and, where needed, referral
