Comments and complaints

If something has not gone the way it should, we would rather hear about it than not. Most things can be sorted out quickly by speaking to the practice manager.

  1. Raise it with us, in person, by phone or in writing. We will acknowledge a written complaint within three working days.
  2. We will investigate and respond in full, normally within ten working days. If it will take longer, we will tell you why and keep you updated.
  3. If you are still unhappy, you can take a private-treatment complaint to the Dental Complaints Service, or raise a concern about a registrant's fitness to practise with the General Dental Council.
