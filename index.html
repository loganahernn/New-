How it works

Four appointments, start to finish

Most new dentures take between three and five weeks. We will confirm your own timetable at the first appointment.

  1. Consultation

    We look at what you have now, talk through the options and the costs, and answer your questions. Free, and there is no pressure to book.

  2. Impressions & measurements

    We take detailed impressions and record how your jaws meet, along with the shade and shape of teeth that will suit you.

  3. Try-in

    You see the denture in wax before it is finished, so the look and the bite can still be changed. Bring someone whose opinion you trust.

  4. Fit & aftercare

    We fit the finished denture and book a review. Adjustments in the settling-in period are included.

Why patients come to us

You deal with the person who makes your denture

At many practices your impressions are posted to a laboratory you never see. Here, the clinical dental technician who takes your measurements is the one at the bench — so nothing is lost in translation, and a small adjustment does not mean waiting a fortnight.

  • Direct access — you can come to us without a referral from a dentist
  • Appointments long enough to get the details right
  • Written estimates before any work begins
  • Repairs handled on site, often the same day
  • Aftercare appointments included with every new denture
